Historical Disaster Data for Silchar and NER:
- June-July 2025: Massive landslides at Jatinga Lampur Area (Dima Hasao) blocked the Lumding-Badarpur hill section with large boulders.
- July 2025: Landslide between Dihaku and Mupa Stations (Kilometer 51/2-3) cut off Silchar, Tripura, and Mizoram.
- September 2026: Soil erosion and track washout occurred on the Jamira (Assam) to Bairabi (Mizoram) section (Katakhal-Bairabi stretch) due to heavy rain.
- 2022-2024 (Recurring): Major floods in Silchar are caused by Barak River embankment breaches, specifically at the Berenga Betukandi Area (The Epicenter).
- 2026 Status: National Highway cross-drainage blockages due to 4-lane highway and flyover construction have stopped natural water flow, causing prolonged urban flooding near highways.
