ITANAGAR & PAPUM PARE DISTRICT: DISASTER & LOGISTICS DATASET

1. RIVER & TERRAIN HYDROLOGICAL LIMITS
* Location Target: Dikrong River System at Sisapathar / Doimukh Gauging Station (CWC Station)
* Danger Level (DL): 86.60 meters
* Warning Level (WL): 85.60 meters
* Highest Flood Level (HFL): 89.07 meters (at Sisapathar) / 121.08 meters (at Doimukh)
* Structural Safety Limit: Hilly terrain infrastructure and urban drainage systems are designed for short-burst runoffs. Retaining walls and riverbank cutoffs fail under high-velocity water surges exceeding DL.
* Key Vulnerability: Riverbanks of the Senki and Pachin rivers (which merge into the Dikrong). Sudden downstream water surges combined with hillside erosion trigger severe structural breaches and flash inundation without traditional embankment protection.

2. RAINFALL TRIGGERS & HYDROMETEOROLOGICAL WARNINGS
* IMD Trigger Categories:
* Short-Burst Intensity Alert: 150.0 mm – 200.0 mm in 3 to 4 hours. Triggers immediate urban flash floods and drainage overflows.
* Very Heavy Rainfall (Orange Alert): 115.6 mm – 204.4 mm in 24 hours. Causes rapid swelling of hilly streams and localized slope instability.
* Extremely Heavy Rainfall / Landslide Alert (Red Alert): >204.5 mm in 24 hours OR prolonged monsoonal rain over 3 consecutive days. Causes catastrophic hill cut-offs, mudslides, and bank cave-ins.
* Soil Saturation Effect: Continuous pre-monsoon and monsoon precipitation saturates steep hill slopes, leading to spontaneous landslides along transit corridors even during moderate rainfall events.

3. HISTORICAL FLOOD & LANDSLIDE TIMELINE (2022–2026)

* May – June 2022 (Poma Infrastructure & Highway Crisis):
* Cause: Continuous high-intensity monsoon rain coupled with widespread hillside failures.
* Extent: Massive landslides blocked NH-415 completely; flood surges destroyed the main water intake pipelines at Poma.
* Impact: Severed municipal drinking water supply to Itanagar Capital Complex for several days and isolated transit routes from Assam.

* June 24–25, 2026 (NEEPCO Colony & Bridge Washout):
* Cause: Extreme 3-hour cloudburst-like rain (6 AM – 9 AM) overwhelming natural drainage basins.
* Extent: Flash floods swept through NEEPCO Colony, swept away a critical highway bridge, destroyed 18 residential quarters, and left 3 individuals missing.

5. LOGISTICS & TRANSPORTATION BLOCKAGES

* Major Arterial Blockages:
* NH-415 (Main Capital Lifeline): Blocked frequently by heavy mudslides between Banderdewa Check Gate and Itanagar (specifically at the Karsingsa block point).
* NH-13 (Trans-Arunachal Highway): Cut off along the Hoj–Potin stretch due to major hill collapse.

* Alternative Emergency Supply Routes:
* Primary Overland Backup: Hollongi–Itanagar Road (via Donyi Polo Airport entrance) when NH-415 is closed.
* Air Relief: Donyi Polo Airport, Itanagar (Hollongi) – deployed for airlifting critical medical aid, rescue teams, and emergency supplies during complete road isolation.

6. DDMA / SDRF PRECAUTIONS & LOCAL COPING MECHANISMS

* Official Infrastructure:
* Papum Pare DDMA and Capital Complex Administration set up emergency relief centers in elevated government institutions (e.g., Modirijo Government Primary School, Donyi Polo Government Primary School).
* SDRF, NDRF, and local authorities deploy earth-moving machinery (JCBs, track excavators) instead of water boats.

* Local Evacuation Practices:
* Immediate self-evacuation from steep hill slopes and low-lying riverbanks to higher, geologically stable ground.
* Deployment of heavy sandbags, temporary wooden retaining structures, and tarpaulins to protect sliding topsoil around residential plots.
* Sourcing drinking water via Public Health Engineering (PHE) tankers and distributing water purification tablets (Halazone/Chlorine) due to broken municipal pipelines.
