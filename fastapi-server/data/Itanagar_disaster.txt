## Complete Date-Wise Disaster Timeline for Itanagar and Arunachal Pradesh (2021–2024)

Below is the comprehensive, date-specific timeline of all floods, landslides, and storms affecting Itanagar and Arunachal Pradesh from 2021 onwards, compiled from official India Meteorological Department (IMD) Disastrous Weather Events reports, Government of Arunachal Pradesh documents, and verified news reports.[1][2][3]

## **2021**

**Source:** IMD Disastrous Weather Events 2021; Arunachal Pradesh SDMP 2022 

### **January–December 2021 (Specific Dates Not Fully Documented in IMD Summary)**

**Landslides & Flash Floods:**
- **Loss of Lives (State-wide, Landslide/Flash Flood/Flood):** 6 deaths reported in Arunachal Pradesh during 2021. 
- **Heavy Rainfall Events:** Multiple intense rainfall events triggered landslides across the state, particularly in Papum Pare (including Itanagar-Naharlagun belt), Lower Subansiri, and East Kameng districts during the monsoon season (June–September 2021). 

**People Affected:**
- State-wide, several thousand people were affected by landslides blocking national highways (NH-15, NH-415) connecting Itanagar to Assam. 
- Multiple villages in Papum Pare district reported landslides damaging houses and public infrastructure. 

**Roads Affected:**
- National Highway 15 and 415 experienced multiple landslide-induced closures during June–September 2021 monsoon. 

**Area Affected:**
- Multiple villages in Papum Pare district; exact area (in hectares) not specified for Itanagar. 

**Reasons for Disasters:**
- Heavy monsoon rainfall triggered landslides in the fragile Himalayan terrain. 
- Unplanned construction and earth-cutting in landslide-prone areas increased vulnerability.

## **2022**

**Source:** IMD Disastrous Weather Events 2022; IMD Monsoon Report 2022; Arunachal Pradesh SDMP 2022; NDMA Regional Conclave 2022 

### **6 April 2022**
- **Location:** Itanagar 
- **Event Type:** Landslide
- **Casualties:** Not specified
- **Damage:** Landslide reported in Itanagar area. 

### **15 May 2022**
- **Location:** Itanagar (Yagamso Colony / near Punjabi Dhaba) 
- **Event Type:** Massive Landslide
- **Time:** Not specified (daytime)
- **Casualties:** 3 deaths (including 1 woman); 3 injured 
- **Damage:** Three people buried alive; rescued by AAPDA Mitra volunteers and local people; significant damage to property. 

### **16 May 2022**
- **Location:** Itanagar (Jollang Road) 
- **Event Type:** Landslide
- **Casualties:** 2 deaths 
- **Damage:** AAPDA Mitra volunteers and SDRF engaged in search and rescue. 

### **28 June 2022**
- **Location:** Itanagar 
- **Event Type:** Landslide
- **Casualties:** 1 death, 3 injured 
- **Damage:** Damage to 3 houses reported. 

### **8–10 August 2022**
- **Location:** Itanagar, Kra Daadi, Tawang 
- **Event Type:** Heavy Rains, Flood & Landslides
- **Casualties:** 5 livestock perished in Itanagar 
- **Damage:** Widespread heavy rainfall triggered floods and landslides. 

### **State-Wide Impact (2022 Monsoon)**
- **Villages Affected:** 308 villages affected due to landslide and flood across Arunachal Pradesh. 
- **People Affected:** 87,344 people impacted state-wide. 
- **Human Lives Lost:** 22 deaths across 6 districts (Itanagar Capital Region - 8, Kurung Kumey - 3, West Kameng - 4, West Siang - 1, Papum Pare - 3, Kra Daadi - 3). 
- **Houses Damaged:** 800 houses damaged state-wide. 
- **Crop Area Damaged:** 96 hectares of cropped area damaged. 
- **Infrastructure Damage:** Damage to roads, power lines, water supply tanks, government buildings, and schools reported across districts. 

**Roads Affected:**
- NH-15 and NH-415 experienced repeated closures due to landslides, particularly during May–August 2022. 
- Margherita-Changlang road, Hoj-Potin road (Papum Pare), Balemu-Bomdila road, and TAH roads in Upper Subansiri and Kurung Kumey were blocked due to heavy rain-triggered landslides. 
- 33 kV power lines damaged in some areas due to landslides. 

**Monsoon Rainfall (2022):**
- Arunachal Pradesh received **deficient** southwest monsoon rainfall in 2022. 

**Reasons for Disasters:**
- Heavy to extremely heavy rainfall events during monsoon triggered landslides in the fragile Himalayan terrain. 
- Unplanned construction and earth-cutting in landslide-prone areas (e.g., Yagamso Colony, Jollang Road) increased vulnerability. 
- Delayed response time due to road blockage, communication delays, and lack of role clarity among response teams.

## **2024**

**Source:** IMD Monsoon Report 2024; Local News Reports (Arunachal Times, India Today NE, Arunachal Observer, The Hindu Business Line)[2][3]

### **26 March 2024**
- **Location:** Itanagar 
- **Event Type:** Record Rainfall
- **Rainfall:** 68.8 mm (breaking the previous March record of 53.1 mm on 24 March 2010) 
- **Damage:** Not specified; this was a record-breaking rainfall event for March. 

### **23 June 2024 (Sunday)**
- **Location:** Itanagar Capital Complex (ICR) and Naharlagun[2][3]
- **Event Type:** Cloudburst / Intense Rainfall leading to landslides and flooding
- **Time:** Approximately 10:30 AM[2][3]
- **Duration:** 40-minute cloudburst 
- **Casualties:** No casualties reported 

**Areas Affected:**
- Kongko village in Chimpu area 
- Banderdewa and Karsingsa 
- Press Colony in Papu Nallah 
- Sood village, Tarajulu village, Nirjuli, and Borum 
- Kime Pakka, Tagin Colony, Modirijo, and Niti Vihar in Itanagar (badly affected) 
- Abo Tani Colony and Niti Vihar area 
- Dree ground in Papu Nallah (badly affected) 
- Residential areas close to Energy Park and Bank Tinali[2]

**People Affected:**
- Out of a population of 3,000 in the affected areas, 300 people were severely affected. 
- Several houses in Niti Vihar, Kime Pakka, Tagin Colony, and Modirijo were reportedly washed away. 
- Around 70 houses were inundated in Tarajuli, Papu Nallah, and Press Colony. 
- Water logging in Niti Vihar area forced many residents to abandon their houses. 
- Rainwater entered many houses in Naharlagun, damaging household belongings. 

**Roads Affected:**
- National Highway 415 between Itanagar and Naharlagun was damaged by the rain.[2]
- Stretches of NH-415 at 6 Mile were partially damaged. 
- Many vehicles were stranded along NH-415 stretches near Doimukh Saturday market. 
- Three people narrowly escaped as the vehicles they were traveling got stuck in the middle of Pare River. 
- Sections of NH-415 were submerged, causing significant disruptions to traffic.[2]

**Infrastructure Damage:**
- The hanging bridge leading to Puroik Colony in Papu Nallah was completely washed away. 
- The Energy Park, located near Indira Gandhi Park, was partially damaged.[2]
- Multiple compound walls and CGI sheets were washed away by landslides. 
- The main water supply line of 400 & 200 mm diameter feeding drinking water supply to Itanagar town was damaged between Zoo road and Ganga Lake, disrupting supply of 11 MLD (million liters per day) water to the township. 

**Relief Efforts:**
- Seven relief camps were established by the district administration to support those affected.[2]
- Rescue and relief efforts were coordinated by district administration officials, including Commissioner Sheta Nagoti Me, the commissioner of Itanagar Municipal Corporation, the State Disaster Response Force, and police personnel.[2]

### **State-Wide Impact (2024 Monsoon, as of July 2024)**
- **People Affected:** Since April 2024, over 72,900 people across 257 villages have been affected by floods and landslides in Arunachal Pradesh. 
- **Human Lives Lost:** Three deaths due to monsoon rains reported since April 2024 – one each in Siang, Upper Subansiri, and Namsai districts. 
- **Villages Affected:** At least 75 villages affected in several districts of the state. 
- **Houses Damaged:** 627 kutcha houses, 51 pucca houses, and 155 huts damaged state-wide. 
- **Infrastructure Damage:**
  - 160 roads damaged/blocked
  - 76 power lines damaged
  - 30 electric poles damaged
  - 3 transformers damaged
  - 9 bridges damaged
  - 11 culverts damaged
  - 147 water supply systems damaged 

**Roads Blocked (State-wide):**
- Tezu-Hayuliang road at Mompani area (Lohit and Anjaw districts) 
- Dari-Chambang and Palin-Taraklengdi roads (Kra Daadi district) via Langdang village 
- Major landslide at Karsingsa block point along NH-415 connecting Itanagar with Banderdewa forced authorities to close the road for safety reasons. 

**Monsoon Rainfall Performance (2024):**
- Arunachal Pradesh received **deficient** southwest monsoon rainfall in 2024. 
- **Seasonal Rainfall:** 1200.0 mm actual vs. 1675.1 mm normal (−28% departure). 
- **Monthly Breakdown:**
  - June: 429.6 mm (−6% departure)
  - July: 366.2 mm (−31% departure)
  - August: 259.1 mm (−24% departure)
  - September: 145.1 mm (−58% departure) — **second-lowest September rainfall since 1901** for Arunachal Pradesh. 

**Reasons for Disasters:**
- Intense rainfall events, despite overall deficient monsoon, triggered localized landslides and flash floods. 
- Illegal earth-cutting and slope modification for construction, despite district administration orders prohibiting such activities, contributed to manmade disasters. 
- Choked drainage systems and human activities (e.g., waste dumping in natural drains) exacerbated flooding in areas like Energy Park. 
- Climate change-induced intensification of extreme rainfall events.

## Consolidated Date-Wise Timeline Summary

### **2021**
- **June–September 2021 (Monsoon):** Multiple landslides and flash floods in Papum Pare (including Itanagar-Naharlagun belt); 6 deaths state-wide; several thousand people affected; NH-15 and NH-415 experienced multiple closures. 

### **2022**
- **6 April 2022:** Landslide in Itanagar. 
- **15 May 2022:** Massive landslide in Yagamso Colony / near Punjabi Dhaba, Itanagar; 3 deaths, 3 injured. 
- **16 May 2022:** Landslide on Jollang Road, Itanagar; 2 deaths. 
- **28 June 2022:** Landslide in Itanagar; 1 death, 3 injured; 3 houses damaged. 
- **8–10 August 2022:** Heavy rains, floods, and landslides in Itanagar, Kra Daadi, Tawang; 5 livestock perished in Itanagar. 
- **State-wide (2022):** 308 villages affected; 87,344 people impacted; 22 deaths; 800 houses damaged; 96 hectares of crops damaged. 

### **2023**
- **15 January to 19 December 2023:** Floods and heavy rains state-wide; 17 deaths in Arunachal Pradesh (5 in pre-monsoon, 12 in monsoon). 
- **March–May 2023 (Pre-Monsoon):** 5 deaths state-wide; several thousand people affected. 
- **June–September 2023 (Monsoon):** 12 deaths state-wide; several thousand people affected; 7 houses completely damaged, 201 partially damaged. 

### **2024**
- **26 March 2024:** Record rainfall in Itanagar (68.8 mm, breaking previous March record). 
- **23 June 2024 (10:30 AM):** Cloudburst/intense rainfall in Itanagar Capital Complex; 300 people severely affected out of 3,000; 70 houses inundated; NH-415 damaged; hanging bridge to Puroik Colony washed away; main water supply line damaged (disrupting 11 MLD water supply).[2][3]
- **April–July 2024 (State-wide):** 72,900 people affected across 257 villages; 3 deaths; 627 kutcha, 51 pucca, 155 huts damaged; 160 roads damaged/blocked; 76 power lines damaged; 9 bridges damaged; 147 water supply systems damaged.
